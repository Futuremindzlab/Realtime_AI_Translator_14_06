Questions

Good to know

What exactly is The OneLingo?

A mobile app for real, spoken conversations across languages. You speak, it detects your language and translates in real time, then speaks the reply back — in a natural voice, or your own cloned one — and automatically flips direction for the other person.

Which languages does it support?

45+ languages and counting, including dedicated accuracy work on Indian languages and right-to-left scripts like Arabic, Hebrew, and Persian — not just the handful most translators stop at.

Is my conversation private?

Yes. Conversations and their audio auto-delete after 90 days, and you can permanently delete your account and every trace of your data at any time, right from the app.

How much does it cost?

Basic will always be free to start. Paid plans are launching alongside the app — join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know pricing, with early-access perks.
