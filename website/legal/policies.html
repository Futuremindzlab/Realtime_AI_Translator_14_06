1.Who this is about

This policy explains what Futureminds Lab LLP ("we", "us") collects when you use The OneLingo, why, who we share it with, and how to have it deleted. It applies to the Android app and its backend services.

2.What we collect

Account
Email address, or phone number for OTP sign-in; your name and country, collected once during onboarding or from Settings — used for sign-in, account recovery, and support/billing correspondence.
Translation content
Recorded audio and the resulting transcript/translation text — to produce the translation you asked for, and to show it in your history.
Usage & preferences
Chosen languages, voice settings, subscription plan — to run the app the way you configured it.
Payment
Subscription status and plan — we never see or store your card/UPI details, our payment partner handles those directly — to grant Plus/Live access and manage billing.
Diagnostics (optional)
On-device app logs, only if you tap "Share Diagnostics" — to debug a problem you reported.

3.Who we share it with

Running the app requires a small set of specialist partners — cloud hosting and authentication, the AI models that power transcription/translation/speech, and a payment processor for subscriptions. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement that lets them use your data only to provide that service to us, never for their own purposes.

We do not sell your data, and do not share it with anyone outside that narrow set of service providers.

4.How long we keep it

Translation history and its audio are automatically and permanently deleted 90 days after creation. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete Account) erases your history, audio, subscription link, and login immediately — this cannot be undone.

Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account — it only stops future billing. Use Delete Account separately if you want your data removed too.

5.Your rights

You can view and change your preferences any time in Settings. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, in-app, with no need to contact us. To ask a question about your data instead, contact us at Admin@futuremindzlab.com.

6.Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7.Grievance officer

1.How billing works

Plus and Live are recurring monthly plans (exact pricing coming soon) that renew automatically every month on the date you first subscribed, until you cancel. Each charge is processed by Razorpay against the payment method you provided at signup.

2.Cancelling

Cancel any time from Settings → Your Plan → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep full access to your plan through the period you already paid for, then move to Basic automatically. No further charges occur after that.

3.Refunds

Because cancellation already stops billing at the natural end of a paid period, and access continues through that period:

  • We do not offer prorated or partial-period refunds for cancelling partway through a billing cycle.
  • If you're charged in error (e.g. a duplicate charge, or a charge after you'd already cancelled), contact us at Admin@futuremindzlab.com within 30 days and we'll investigate and refund confirmed errors in full.
  • Refunds, where approved, are issued to the original payment method via Razorpay and can take 5–7 business days to appear, per your bank/UPI provider.
Adjust this section to match your actual policy — as built, the app only supports cancel-at-cycle-end with no automatic proration. If you want to offer a cooling-off refund window instead (common for a first-time subscriber), say so and this page — and the cancellation code — can both be updated to match.

4.Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Razorpay retries it automatically. If it keeps failing, your subscription is halted and your account moves to Basic — no partial charge is taken for that period.

5.Contact

Billing questions: Admin@futuremindzlab.com. Include your account email and, if relevant, the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt.
