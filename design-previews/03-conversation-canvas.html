Design direction C

Conversation Canvas

Designed around two people rather than one phone screen. A split face-to-face view puts the other speaker's text upside-down toward them while a centre dock holds the mic and hand-off controls; the full exchange also reads back as a chat transcript. This is the direction that makes your conversation mode feel native.
